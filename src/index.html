How to use
  1. Pick the guest's natural starting level and the level they want to end at.
  2. Choose a tone, or open Custom for bespoke tonal families and the colour wheel selector.
  3. Add the guest's % of grey, and their pattern, texture and density, plus whether you're applying on or off the scalp.
  4. Read the formula. Open “How this formula was created” to follow the steps.

How the rings work

Centre is the softest reflection, the middle ring is standard, and toward the edge Pure Pigment is added for intensity. The rings show where every tone sits, weakest inside to strongest outside: Light Pure Tones, then Pure Pigments, then Dark Pure Tones. The set that matches your shade is bright. Tap a Pure Tone on its ring to use it strong, or a Pure Pigment on its own.

    How this formula was created
        Formulation chart

        Shaded cells: going darker with 5 or 10 Volume. Unshaded: going lighter with 20 Volume. X: hair must be prelightened; Full Spectrum Deep is not recommended. Two shades in one cell means equal parts. † Natural levels 7–10 are not on the printed chart; these rows are derived from the going-lighter equation and the one-shade-above rule for going darker.

        Source

        Full Spectrum Deep™ Systems training deck (GLOBAL, 10.1.26), Participant Leaflet (8/2026), Aveda Hair Color Professional Reference Guide (9.12.25) for the Botanical Repair™ colour treatment and porosity guidance, and the Aveda Professional Treatments class deck: formulation chart, mixing and processing guidelines, warm and cool customization charts, Vibrant Red series, base customization and grey coverage guidelines.
