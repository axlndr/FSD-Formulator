Colour wheel

Tap or drag anywhere on the wheel. Where you land around the wheel chooses the tone; how far from the centre chooses the strength. Centre is the softest reflection, the middle is standard, and toward the edge Pure Pigment is added for more intensity.

The rings show where every tone sits and how strong it is, weakest inside to strongest outside: Light Pure Tones, then Pure Pigments, then Dark Pure Tones. The set that matches your shade is bright. Tap a Pure Tone on its ring to use it strong, or a Pure Pigment to use it on its own.
